How Long Does Invisalign Take? A Complete Guide for Patients in Porur

Reviewed by Dr. Rishikumar. A, BDS, MDS — dentist in Porur & dental clinic in Kolapakkam, Chennai. Everything you need to know about Invisalign timelines, costs, and results before you book your consultation.

Quick Answer

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Invisalign treatment at Smile Craft Dental Clinic in Porur typically takes between 6 and 18 months, depending on how complex your alignment issues are. Mild crowding or spacing cases can be resolved in as little as 6 months. Most adult patients with moderate alignment issues complete treatment in 12 to 15 months.

What is Invisalign and why do patients in Porur choose it?

Invisalign is a system of custom-made, removable clear plastic aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the correct position. Unlike traditional braces, there are no metal brackets or wires — the aligners are nearly invisible when worn, which is why working professionals and adults across Porur, Mugalivakkam, and Madanandapuram prefer them.

Each set of aligners is worn for approximately 1 to 2 weeks before moving on to the next. You visit your dentist in Porur roughly every 6 to 8 weeks for a progress check. There are no dietary restrictions since the aligners are removed before eating — one of the biggest practical advantages over fixed braces.

Factors that affect your Invisalign timeline

No two cases are alike. Dr. Rishikumar at our Porur clinic considers the following before estimating your treatment time:

  • check_circleSeverity of misalignment — mild crowding resolves faster than significant bite correction
  • check_circleCompliance — aligners must be worn 20–22 hours every day. Removing them too often is the most common reason treatment takes longer than expected
  • check_circleAge — adults typically have slower tooth movement than teenagers, so treatment may take slightly longer
  • check_circleNumber of aligners in your plan — your treatment plan is mapped digitally before you begin, showing exactly how many trays are needed
  • check_circleRefinements — some patients need additional aligner sets toward the end of treatment to perfect the final result

Invisalign vs metal braces — a quick comparison for Porur patients

Both work well, but they suit different patients. Here is a quick guide from Dr. Rishikumar at our Porur dental clinic:

  • check_circleChoose Invisalign if you are an adult, need mild to moderate correction, work in a client-facing role, or have an upcoming event like a wedding
  • check_circleChoose metal braces if your case is complex, you are getting treatment for a child or teenager, or budget is the primary concern
  • check_circleConsider ceramic braces if you want less visibility than metal braces but cannot budget for Invisalign

What happens at your first Invisalign appointment in Porur?

When you visit Smile Craft Dental Clinic as a new Invisalign patient, here is exactly what to expect:

  • check_circleA full dental examination to confirm your gums and teeth are healthy enough for orthodontic treatment
  • check_circleDigital impressions — no messy putty mould. A 3D scan is taken in minutes
  • check_circleA ClinCheck preview — you see a digital simulation of your final result before agreeing to treatment
  • check_circleA transparent treatment plan with the exact number of aligners, estimated duration, and full costs
  • check_circleYour first set of aligners is ready in approximately 2 to 3 weeks after your impressions are taken

Life with Invisalign — practical tips from our Porur patients

  • check_circleAlways remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than plain water
  • check_circleBrush your teeth before reinserting aligners after a meal — food trapped under aligners causes cavities
  • check_circleSet a phone timer each morning to track your 22 hours of wear
  • check_circleKeep your aligner case with you at all times — lost aligners slow your treatment
  • check_circleSwitch to the next tray at night so any initial tightness is felt while you sleep

Frequently Asked Questions — Invisalign in Porur

Is Invisalign faster than metal braces?

For mild to moderate cases, Invisalign can be faster — completing in 6 to 12 months versus 18 to 24 months for traditional braces. However, this depends entirely on compliance. Metal braces work 24 hours a day regardless of patient habits. Invisalign is only as fast as your discipline allows.

Can I get Invisalign if I have had braces before?

Yes — in most cases. Many adults come to our Porur clinic after childhood metal braces whose teeth have slightly shifted over time. Invisalign is an excellent option for these relapse cases. Dr. Rishikumar will assess your current alignment and recommend the right treatment at your consultation.
