Dental Implants in Kolapakkam: What to Expect Before, During and After

Reviewed by Dr. Rishikumar. A, BDS, MDS — dental clinic in Kolapakkam & dentist in Porur, Chennai. A complete, honest guide to dental implants — from your first consultation through to a fully restored smile.

Quick Answer

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. Once it bonds with the bone — a process called osseointegration — a crown is attached on top, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural one. Unlike dentures, implants are permanent. Unlike bridges, they do not require grinding down neighbouring healthy teeth.

info

Dental implants are not just cosmetic — they prevent bone loss in the jaw that occurs whenever a tooth is missing. The longer you wait after losing a tooth, the more bone shrinks, which can make future implant placement more complex.

Are you a good candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults are suitable candidates. At our dental clinic in Kolapakkam, Dr. Rishikumar checks the following before recommending implants:

  • check_circleSufficient bone density — the jawbone must be thick enough to anchor the implant securely. A bone graft can be done first if needed
  • check_circleHealthy gums — active gum disease must be treated before implant surgery
  • check_circleControlled systemic health — conditions like uncontrolled diabetes can affect healing, but well-managed diabetes is not a barrier
  • check_circleNon-smoker or willing to quit — smoking significantly increases implant failure risk
  • check_circleCompleted jaw growth — implants are recommended for adults only, as bone growth must be complete

The dental implant procedure — step by step

Understanding the process helps reduce anxiety. Here is exactly what happens at Smile Craft Dental Clinic in Kolapakkam:

  • check_circleStep 1 — Consultation: Digital X-ray and 3D scan to assess bone volume and plan implant position precisely
  • check_circleStep 2 — Implant placement: A small titanium screw is placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia. The procedure takes 30 to 60 minutes per implant
  • check_circleStep 3 — Healing period: 3 to 6 months for the implant to fuse with the bone. A temporary crown is provided during this time so you are never without a tooth
  • check_circleStep 4 — Abutment placement: A small connector piece is attached to the implant — a minor procedure done under local anaesthesia
  • check_circleStep 5 — Crown fitting: A custom-made permanent crown is fitted. It is colour-matched to your natural teeth so it blends in completely

Recovery and aftercare

Most patients at our Kolapakkam clinic resume normal activities the day after implant surgery. Here is what to expect during the healing period:

  • check_circleMild swelling and soreness for 2 to 3 days — managed with standard paracetamol or ibuprofen
  • check_circleEat soft foods for the first week — no hard or crunchy foods around the implant site
  • check_circleAvoid smoking for at least 2 weeks after surgery — smoking doubles the risk of implant failure
  • check_circleBrush gently around the implant site — Dr. Rishikumar will show you the correct technique
  • check_circleAttend your scheduled follow-up appointments so healing progress can be monitored

Implants vs bridges vs dentures — which is right for you?

Patients at our Kolapakkam dental clinic frequently ask this. Here is Dr. Rishikumar's honest comparison:

  • check_circleImplants — the gold standard. Permanent, prevent bone loss, no impact on neighbouring teeth. Highest upfront cost but lowest lifetime cost
  • check_circleBridges — fixed, look natural, lower cost. Requires grinding down the two teeth on either side of the gap. Does not prevent bone loss under the bridge
  • check_circleRemovable dentures — lowest cost. Can feel less stable over time as bone shrinks. Requires daily removal and cleaning

For a single missing tooth in a patient with healthy bone, Dr. Rishikumar almost always recommends an implant as the long-term best option. You can also explore our Porur dental clinic for implant consultations at our flagship branch.

Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants in Kolapakkam

How long do dental implants last?

The titanium implant post can last a lifetime with good oral hygiene and regular check-ups. The crown on top typically lasts 15 to 20 years before needing replacement. Compared to bridges (10 to 15 years) or dentures (5 to 8 years), implants offer the best long-term value.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

The surgery is performed under local anaesthesia, so you will not feel pain during the procedure. Post-surgery discomfort is mild and typically resolves within 2 to 3 days. Most patients at our Kolapakkam clinic describe the recovery as easier than they expected — comparable to a tooth extraction.
